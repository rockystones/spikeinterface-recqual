Success criterion: a three-layer metric stack (threshold-crossing, per-sorter
quality, multi-sorter agreement) validated longitudinally across the cohort and
at least two probe types, with the agreement *structure* reported as the
headline longitudinal metric.
Approach: scratch-first SpikeInterface pipeline; per-session computation under
the aggregation rule (D-002); snippet and continuous regimes handled as equals
(D-003); consensus never collapsed to a single sorting.
