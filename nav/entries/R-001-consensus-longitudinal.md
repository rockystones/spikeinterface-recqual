What ran: 48 era-spanning stems x 4 sorters, agreement graph per stem.
Outcome: median pairwise match 0.27-0.34 on every array incl. Rocky I2; dead
arrays give 36->1->0->0 and 210->1->1->0 ladders; declines land on the arrays
that died (Nigel Ant rho -0.83, Rocky I1 Post -0.89).
Interpretation: the consensus ladder is a spike-validity metric no single
sorter provides; count-based dispersion proxies are retired as trends.
